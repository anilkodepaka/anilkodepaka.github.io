## A little about this site

I'm a cloud and platform engineer with a passion for building things, solving problems, and learning along the way.

I've spent much of my career working with enterprise infrastructure, cloud platforms, Kubernetes, automation, and more recently cloud-native data and analytics platforms.

This website is my little space on the internet to share some of that journey.

I'll be writing about things I work with, things I'm learning, projects I've worked on, and occasionally the problems I've had to figure out along the way.

Not everything here will be a polished tutorial or the "perfect" way of doing something. Sometimes it's simply **what worked for me, what I learned, and what I would do differently next time.**

If something here helps you solve a problem, learn something new, or gives you another way of looking at a problem, then the site has served its purpose.
