**Client:** Takeda, Switzerland  
**Role:** Platform Architect  
**Period:** January 2026 – March 2026  
**Technology:** AWS, Amazon EKS, Kubernetes, Posit/RStudio

### The project

Takeda had an existing RStudio environment that had been in place for around five years.

The objective was to modernize the platform and provide a new, secure and maintainable environment while migrating existing users and their projects.

### My role

I was responsible for taking the platform through the complete delivery lifecycle — from architecture and planning through implementation, validation, production rollout and operational handover.

### What I did

- Worked with the Posit vendor to develop the new platform architecture.
- Reviewed and refined the architecture with the internal architecture team.
- Designed and built the AWS EKS and Kubernetes-based platform.
- Installed and configured the Posit platform.
- Planned the migration of existing users and their projects.
- Worked with QA on IQ/OQ documentation and validation activities.
- Coordinated formal change-management activities for production rollout.
- Worked with infrastructure, security, data science and QA stakeholders.
- Created technical and operational documentation.
- Conducted transition and knowledge-transfer sessions with the operations team.

### Outcome

A new cloud-native Posit platform was delivered and transitioned to operations, providing a modern foundation for enterprise analytics and data science workloads while meeting the organization's security, validation and change-management requirements.
